## 1. Card titles are clipped at every desktop width — publish blocker

The ten card fronts are the new direction, and on a desktop they are wrong.

`styles/board.css:49` sets `.board { columns: 4 }`, and the breakpoints at
lines 191–196 key off **viewport** width. But the board is capped at roughly
810px regardless of viewport, so on desktop it is always four columns inside
810px — cards land at ~188px wide holding a 30px display serif.

Measured, `scrollWidth > offsetWidth` on the card `h2`:

| Viewport | Board width | Columns | Card width | Titles clipped |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1512 | 807 | 4 | 187 | 9 of 10 |
| 1280 | 826 | 4 | 188 | 9 of 10 |
| 1100 | 772 | 3 | 241 | 1 of 10 |
| 820 | 739 | 2 | 362 | none |
| 375 | 335 | 1 | 335 | none |

At 1280 the fronts read "Guest Experien", "Quality Assuranc", "Property
Details" on two cramped lines, the caption wraps to three lines, and the
photograph is squeezed to a sliver. At 820 — the tablet layout — the same
card is exactly what was designed: full photograph, title on one line,
"4 questions · flip to explore" on one line.

The design is right. The column count is wrong for the width the board
actually gets.

**Suggested fix, one line.** Drive the count off the board's real width
instead of the viewport by using column-width rather than column-count:

    .board { columns: 360px; column-gap: 24px; }

and delete the 1400/1100/820 breakpoints. In an 810px board that resolves to
two columns of ~380px — the layout that already looks right — and it follows
the container automatically if the board is ever widened.

**The alternative** is to let the board break out wider than the text column
on desktop (say 1180px) and keep three columns at ~370px. That is a bigger
design change and touches the left rail.

## 2. The Quick Scan is nine questions; the site says ten

`read.js:37` sets `LAST_Q = 9`, and the accessible progress line renders
"Project, question 1 of 9". Meanwhile "Ten questions, mostly pictures"
appears on the home page, `/about/`, `/read/` itself, `/glamping-show/`,
three journal pages, and the `/read/` meta description, og:description and
twitter:description.

Ten pairs with Ten Point, so I assume ten is the intent and the scan is one
short — but that is a content decision, not a bug I should pick.

## 3. The AI read does not ship — the draft copy does

`config.js:51` has `MODEL_PROXY_URL: ''`. Locally the Worker is running, so
the board's ledger shows the model's prose. In production it will not: every
visitor gets the template read from `standardread.js`, which still carries
the header "DRAFT COPY pending Kenny's pass."

That is the copy a stranger judges us on, and it includes lines like
"Your answers put this project in the middle of the paper work."
(`standardread.js:31`). Same for the ten `FIELD_NOTES` in `app.js`.

Not a defect — it is the known state — but worth saying plainly before a
push, because the polished version on screen locally is not the shipping one.
