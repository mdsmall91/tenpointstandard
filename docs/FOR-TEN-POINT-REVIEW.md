# tenpointstandard.com — what we need your words on

The site is live. Everything below is already on it and working; this is
the list of places where the *words* are a stand-in and need somebody at
Ten Point to say it properly.

Nothing here is broken. It is the difference between copy that sounds
right and copy that is right, and only you can tell them apart.

---

## Start here

**https://tenpointstandard.com**

- **Quick Scan** — six questions, about ninety seconds, no email. It asks
  who the project is *for*, not what is being built, and it scores
  nothing.
- **Full Assessment** — ten photographic cards over the forty questions.
  Flip any card, any order. The eleventh card is the ledger.

---

## 1. The ten field notes — the most important thing on this list

One line sits on the back of each card, under the answers. It is the only
place on the site where somebody hears a builder talking rather than a
form asking. **These were written to hold the space, not to be final.**

If a line is wrong, say what you would actually say on a site walk.

| # | Point | The line as it reads today |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Property Details | The survey tells you where the lines are. The site walk tells you what the lines are hiding. |
| 02 | Capital Strategy | Money that is close is not money that is committed. A lender can tell the difference on the first read. |
| 03 | Regulatory Approvals | Most projects we see do not lose time at the permit counter. They lose it waiting on a study nobody scheduled. |
| 04 | Guest Experience | Every design argument we have ever sat through was really an argument about who the guest is. |
| 05 | Design | A drawing set is not done when it looks finished. It is done when a builder can price it without calling you. |
| 06 | Procurement | Knowing the lead time does not reserve the slot. A deposit reserves the slot. |
| 07 | Schedule | A schedule built backward from opening day is a wish. Build it forward from the approvals. |
| 08 | Cost Certainty | The budget nobody outside the project has checked is the budget that moves after the money commits. |
| 09 | Quality Assurance | Quality is decided by whoever walks the site on a Tuesday, not by what the contract says. |
| 10 | Opening Readiness | The building is the easy half. The first ninety days of operating it is the half that gets skipped. |

Two of these say **"most projects we see"** and **"every design argument
we have ever sat through."** That is Ten Point's experience being claimed
on Ten Point's behalf. If it is not what you have seen, it has to change.

## 2. The written reads

At the end of both lanes the site writes a short read of the project back
to the person. It is assembled from their answers, so it is accurate, but
the sentences are drafted.

Worth doing: run the Quick Scan twice with different answers and read
what comes back. It is the first thing a stranger judges us on.

## 3. Four sentences on /about/

One per project — Lagom Retreat, KOA Fredericksburg, Outdoorsy Hill
Country, Austin Moto Adventures. They currently say less rather than
claim a problem that was solved. If there is a real one-line story for
each, they should carry it.

## 4. The photographs and their credits

Each card carries a credit on the open panel, naming the project. Please
check every one is a job Ten Point actually built, and that nothing
implies otherwise. A photograph with the wrong credit is a licensing
problem for a licensed general contractor, not a design detail.

## What the site deliberately never says

This matters more than it sounds, and it is worth knowing before you read
anything the site writes.

Nothing on the site gives a **cost**, a **schedule**, a **yield or
occupancy figure**, or a **ruling on whether a jurisdiction will approve
anything**. That boundary is enforced three times over, including a final
pattern check that throws away any sentence that breaches it.

It is why a read will say "it is worth looking into whether the county
allows this use" and never "the county will approve this."

If you ever want the site to say one of those things, it is a decision
to make deliberately, not a wording tweak.
