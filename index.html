A readiness assessment for outdoor hospitality projects.

The Ten Point Standard is the framework Ten Point Services uses to judge whether a campground, RV resort, or glamping resort is ready to move forward. The Field Guide is the self-assessment version: forty yes or no questions across ten points, scored out of one hundred.

Common questions

What is the Ten Point Standard?
The Ten Point Standard is a readiness framework for outdoor hospitality development. It covers ten points: property details, capital strategy, regulatory approvals, guest experience, design, procurement, schedule, cost certainty, quality assurance, and opening readiness. Each point carries four questions and a weighted share of one hundred points.
Who should take the Field Guide assessment?
Owners and developers planning a campground, RV resort, glamping resort, or other outdoor hospitality property. It is written for people early enough to still change the plan, from raw land through pre-construction.
How long does the assessment take?
About five minutes. Forty yes or no questions across ten screens, with no account to create.
What do I get at the end?
A score out of one hundred, a verdict on the stage your project is actually in, a read on which critical gates are still open, the findings your answers point to, and three priority actions. You can have the scorecard emailed to you.
What does my score mean?
Zero to thirty-nine is Align: market, land, and money are still open questions. Forty to sixty-nine is Design: the idea holds, and the risk sits in entitlements, drawings, procurement, and pricing. Seventy to one hundred is Build: the seams are closed, and the work is holding schedule, budget, and quality through construction. An open critical gate can pull the verdict below the raw score.
Does the assessment cost anything?
No. The Field Guide is free. An email address is needed only if you want the scorecard sent to you.

The Field Guide is published by Ten Point Services. It is a diagnostic, not a feasibility study, an appraisal, or professional advice on any specific project.
